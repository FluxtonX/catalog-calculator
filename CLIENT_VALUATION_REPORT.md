## 3. How the Calculation Works (In Simple Steps)

This matches your **Phase 1 Formula In One Line**:

$$\text{Public Streams} \rightarrow \text{Monthly Run-Rate} \rightarrow \text{Geo Payout} \rightarrow \text{Annualize } (\times 12) \rightarrow \text{Sum Platforms} \rightarrow \text{Apply } 6\text{x}/8\text{x}/10\text{x}$$

### Step 1: Track Age & Run-Rate
- For each of the artist's Top 10 tracks, the system calculates track age from original release date.
- Applies your maturity factors ($100\%$ for 0–3 months, $85\%$ for 4–12 months, $65\%$ for 13–36 months, $50\%$ for $>36$ months) to estimate current monthly stream run-rate.

### Step 2: Geo-Weighted Payout Rates
- Uses regional rates based on listener geography:
  - **Spotify**: $\$0.0042$ (US/CA/UK/AU), $\$0.0036$ (EU West), $\$0.0018$ (LATAM), $\$0.0022$ (Asia), $\$0.0016$ (ROW)
  - **Apple Music**: $\$0.0080$ (Tier 1), $\$0.00675$ (ROW)
  - **YouTube**: $\$0.0020$ (Tier 1), $\$0.0010$ (ROW)
- Produces the estimated monthly revenue per track.

### Step 3: Annualize & Multiples
- Multiplies monthly streaming earnings by **12** to establish the estimated annual earning power.
- Applies standard CFA multiples: **6x**, **8x** (Headline value), and **10x**.
